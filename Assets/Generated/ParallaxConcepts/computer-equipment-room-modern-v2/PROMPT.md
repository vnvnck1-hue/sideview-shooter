입력: `Assets/Generated/ParallaxConcepts/computer-equipment-room-v1/computer_equipment_room.png`

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: finished wide pixel-art side-scrolling game environment concept. Edit the referenced old-fashioned computer equipment room into a convincingly MODERN, contemporary computer-dense network operations and server-control room. Preserve only the panoramic ~2.49:1 composition, close back wall, shallow side-on perspective, narrow continuous walking deck at the bottom, dense equipment layering, foreground/rear distinction, and crisp substantial clustered pixel-art style. Radically redesign ALL equipment and architecture; do not merely recolor the old room. Remove every curved CRT, beige monitor, tape reel, analog dial board, rusty riveted column, hazard stripe, hanging cage lamp, exposed messy cable and grungy machinery. Replace with varied charcoal server racks with perforated or dark glass doors, clearly structured rackmount storage/network units, enclosed UPS cabinets, neat cable management and cable trays, numerous slim flat wide monitors on articulated arms with a few portrait screens, compact operator desks, thin keyboards and mice, small workstation towers, and ergonomic mesh chairs. At least twelve legibly distinct flat displays, but avoid a monotonous video wall. Modern clean matte light-grey wall/fascia panels, dark metal rack housings, restrained ventilation grilles, integrated linear LED strips and raised-access floor with subtle seams. Contemporary practical technology, NOT sci-fi holograms. Asymmetrical groups and varied silhouettes; near desks partly occlude quieter rear racks. Keep narrow walkable strip and compressed depth, no central tunnel or deep floor. Rear equipment is cooler darker slate with subdued detail; near operator hardware is neutral graphite/light grey with legible form and restrained teal-blue data on a few screens. Screens vary in brightness; no giant blue glow, neon trim, RGB gaming lights, logos, readable text, character, HUD, watermark or border. Coherent few-pixel top/side surfaces and contact shadows at one eye level. Material shapes and functional details remain clear at game scale. One complete full-frame illustration, not diagrams or separated panels.
```

## 초기 준비안 (미사용)

첫 생성 시도가 사용량 한도로 실패했을 때 보존한 프롬프트다. 이번 결과의 실제 프롬프트는 위 본문이며, 아래 준비안은 실행 입력으로 사용되지 않았다.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: a redesigned finished environment concept for an existing pixel-art side-scrolling game.
Input image 1 is the previous computer-room concept to REDESIGN. The user rejected its old-fashioned technology and wants a MODERN computer-filled room instead.
Keep: the panoramic approximately 2.49:1 canvas, shallow side-on game viewpoint, close back wall, narrow continuous low walking deck, dense concentration of computer equipment, clear overlapping front and rear groups, and crisp chunky pixel-art rendering. No characters or HUD. Do NOT preserve the obsolete equipment identities or the rusty architecture.
Replace the ENTIRE visual era convincingly, not just repaint the screens. Create a contemporary network operations / server control room, plausible modern hardware, NOT retrofuturism and NOT a far-future spaceship hologram bridge.
Remove ALL curved CRT tubes and thick beige CRT bezels, green phosphor scanline displays, open tape reels, oversized analog toggle boards, cage lamps, exposed giant rivets, chipped rusty iron pillars, yellow-black industrial stripes and dingy overhead machinery.
Modern equipment: dense charcoal-black standard-height server racks with perforated or dark smoked-glass doors, horizontal rackmount computing/storage units and neat network patch panels; clusters of slim widescreen flat monitors on VESA arms, with a few vertically oriented displays; thin keyboards and mice on clean compact laminate/metal operator desks; contemporary small workstation towers and enclosed UPS cabinets; ergonomic mesh-back chairs. At least a dozen clearly distinguishable flat displays across the room plus substantial racks of computer hardware. A mix of monitor aspect ratios, rack heights and functional units; not a uniform video-wall wallpaper. Show small abstract dashboard windows, charts and muted blue/teal interface elements, not legible text.
Architecture also changes: clean matte warm-grey/light-grey composite wall/column covers, dark powder-coated rack housings, recessed cable trays with neatly bundled cables, ventilation grilles, thin white linear LED luminaires, a shallow raised-access floor with restrained panel seams. Minimal ordinary wear at contact points, NO all-over grime, rust or decorative bolt noise. Modern does not mean featureless white boxes: model enclosure seams, chassis handles, rubber feet, ventilation, glass thickness and desk construction with coherent pixel clusters.
Composition: varied asymmetrical banks of near operator workstations partially overlap the quieter rear server arrays. Most near monitors and desks sit along the back edge of the walking deck; leave the narrow front walking strip continuous. One or two grouped workstation angles reveal just a little side/top surface at a shared plausible eye level. Large calm wall panels and dark gaps separate dense equipment groups. Avoid a deep aisle converging toward a central vanishing point, a cavernous data center, high-angle isometric view or vast floor acreage. No tiny floating boxed room in black space.
EXACTLY TWO planned background motion groups: BACK = recessed wall, attached rear server racks/screens, rear lighting and their own cable runs. FRONT = near console desks/monitors/chairs/equipment, near structural fascia/columns/lighting, walking deck and lower face. The uneven front silhouettes expose parts of the rear racks for subtle parallax. Do not draw cables visibly joining independently moving groups. No separate third foreground plane; any lower trim belongs to the front group.
Color/value hierarchy: rear muted deep slate blue and charcoal, deliberately compressed small-detail contrast; near matte light grey, neutral graphite and restrained warm-grey planes with clear silhouette/contact shadows. Screens have varied dimness; most rear screens are subdued, only a few near operator displays brighter. No uniformly brilliant blue rectangles, neon outlines, RGB gaming lights, green tint washing the room or heavy bloom. Preserve material colors instead of recoloring every interactive-looking object yellow.
Rendering remains the established hand-designed chunky pixel-art concept language with crisp edges and organized stepped shades; NEVER photorealistic, smooth 3D, painterly or blurred. Modernize design, not the rendering medium.
Output ONE complete finished room illustration, no comparison layout, no diagrams, no labels, no border or watermark. This is a flattened concept, not separated layer files.
```
